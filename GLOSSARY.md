# ShowVilla

ShowVilla turns supported story facts into short, comic-style retellings.

## Language

**Story brief**:
A summary, scene or idea supplied by the user.

**Episode**:
A named part of a particular show and adaptation.

**Story facts**:
Details established by the story brief or checked sources for the requested episode.

**Spoiler limit**:
The latest story information the user permits the retelling to reveal.

**Comic story**:
A five-panel retelling with two closing questions and a short TLDR.
