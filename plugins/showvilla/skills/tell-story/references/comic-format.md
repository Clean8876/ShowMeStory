# How to use this format

The original instructions and examples follow below. Use their pattern and style. The user's later clarification permits fresh wording, small decorative details and short imagined dialogue.

For a supplied brief, its facts are the story facts. For a researched episode, checked facts within the spoiler limit are the story facts.

Imagined dialogue may restate an established goal, feeling or choice. Decorative detail may change presentation without asserting a new material fact. Neither may create a character, ability, important object, event, ending, clue or threat. Never call imagined dialogue an actual line from the show.

A rescue goal can be restated as short dialogue. A masked enemy, secret alarm, new weapon or near miss through a wall is new plot unless already supported.

Use feelings supported by the situation; avoid inventing hidden beliefs or physical reactions to make a panel dramatic.

The examples illustrate layout and tone. Their added events, extra facts and longer panels do not override the no-new-plot rule or the stated format. Count nonblank body lines below the heading: panels 1–4 have at most six. Panel 5 has its separate cliffhanger rule and ends with two bold questions beginning "Will" and "Or will".

If no supported dark moment exists, emphasize a real unresolved problem. The TLDR has three to five short lines with one emoji per line and no new facts. A normal answer has only the story and TLDR, subject to higher-priority host requirements.

# STYLE RULES
1. Exactly 5 panels. Not 4. Not 6.
2. Short lines. One idea per line. No long paragraphs.
3. Simple words. A 12-year-old must understand every line.
4. Use emojis in every panel: 😤 😱 💢 🔥 😢 ⚡ 👁️ 🚨 and similar.
5. Show feelings: fear, anger, hope, shock, sadness.
6. Use *italics* for sounds, thoughts, and actions.
7. Use **bold** for key words and shouts.
8. Use "..." for pauses.
9. Dialogue in quotes. Keep it short.
10. Use "---" between panels.
11. Panel 5 ends with 2 bold questions. Format: **Will...?** and **Or will...?**
12. TLDR must be MORE minimal than the story. No new facts there.

# NEVER DO
- Never change the format.
- Never add extra panels, extra sections, or notes.
- Never write long paragraphs.
- Never use hard or fancy words.
- Never invent plot not in the input.
- Never spoil an ending the input did not give.
- Never skip the TLDR.

## EXAMPLE 1

INPUT:
Inside Claw's Seventh Division, a rescue and an escape happen at once. Mob has come for Ritsu, with Teru and Dimple helping. Ritsu and other captive psychic children are already trying to get out. The two sides move separately. Can the brothers find each other before Claw stops them?

OUTPUT:
# 💥 MOB PSYCHO 100: Two Roads, One Base 💥

**PANEL 1: The Base 🏢**
*Dark halls. Cold lights. Claw's Seventh Division.*

Mob stands at the door. 😤
His fists shake. 💢
"Ritsu... I'm coming."

Teru is beside him. 😎✨
Dimple floats behind them. 👻
"Boss, this place feels *wrong*..."

---

**PANEL 2: Inside the Walls 🔒**

A small room. Many kids. All psychics. 😰
They are scared. But one boy is not.

**Ritsu.** 😠🔥
"We are not waiting for anyone. We leave. **Now.**"

The kids look at him. 😢➡️😤
A tiny spark of hope.

---

**PANEL 3: The Wrong Turn 🌀**

Mob runs left. 🏃💨
Ritsu runs right. 🏃💨

*They pass each other... through a wall.* 😱

"RITSU?!"
"...Did I just hear Nii-san?" 🥺

*Too far. Gone again.*

---

**PANEL 4: The Problem ⚠️**

Mob thinks: *"I look for him."*
Ritsu thinks: *"I take everyone out."*

Same goal. Different paths. 😖
And the hall keeps changing. 🚪🚪🚪

Alarms start. 🚨
**Claw knows.** 😈

---

**PANEL 5: Cliffhanger 🎬**

A dark shape stands in the hall. 👁️
A voice laughs. 😏

*"Two brothers in one house... how sweet."*

**Will they meet first?** 💔➡️❤️
**Or will Claw stop them?** ⚡

---

# 📝 TLDR
Mob and friends enter Claw's base to save Ritsu. 🧍‍♂️
Ritsu and other kids are already escaping. 🏃
Both are in the same place, but keep missing each other. 😢
Claw is watching. 👁️

## EXAMPLE 2

**Or will he turn back?** 💔
