# Choose what to reveal

For a named episode, never reveal events after that episode unless the user explicitly permits them. When no episode is named, use episode 1 as the subject and boundary.

By default, make an opening teaser: establish the people, their aims and the immediate problem, while leaving the episode's ending and major twists hidden. If the user explicitly requests a full recap or the ending, include supported outcomes within the requested boundary. Respect any stricter user limits.

For a supplied summary, use only facts in the summary. Do not reveal an outcome the summary does not give. A cliffhanger question must follow from the supported situation and must not imply an invented event, twist, villain or result.

Keep a short internal list of allowed facts. Research may uncover later or forbidden information, but it must not enter any panel, dialogue, sound, title, question or TLDR.
